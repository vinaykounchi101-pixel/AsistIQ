## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a restorative, orderly, and calm working environment for enterprise IT operations. Traditional IT service desks induce fatigue through hyper-dense tables, aggressive alarm-state neons, and utilitarian friction. In contrast, this design system applies Nordic minimalist principles: spatial clarity, natural balance, purposeful restraint, and serene warmth.

The target audience comprises system administrators, enterprise service agents, DevOps specialists, and corporate end-users seeking assistance. The interface communicates stability, quiet competence, and psychological safety. 

Key stylistic tenets:
- **Nordic Minimalism & Spatial Generosity:** Density is tamed via intentional negative space and structured horizontal registers rather than cramped table grids.
- **Organic Subtlety:** Surfaces rely on matte textures, delicate neutral borders, and natural light absorption rather than high-contrast cyber glows, heavy gradients, or glossy glassmorphism.
- **De-escalated Color Theory:** Critical statuses inform without panic; alert states use muted earthen pigments rather than flashing neons.

## Colors

The palette is tuned exclusively for sRGB fidelity, relying on soft, desaturated Scandinavian tones that reduce optic strain during prolonged shifts:

- **Primary (`#5E6C9E` - Nordic Periwinkle):** A measured, cool slate-periwinkle conveying structural reliability for active interactive states, primary actions, and selected tabs.
- **Secondary (`#9B7E9F` - Heather Lavender):** A dusty, contemplative purple-slate used for secondary tags, categorical classifications, and contextual highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#7693A6` - Muted Powder Blue):** An atmospheric coastal tone designated for informational accents, progress meters, and neutral data anchors.
- **Neutral (`#6C727A` - Slate Sandstone):** A balanced gray ground with mineral undertones ensuring WCAG AAA legibility for body copy against light tinted backdrops.

### Surface and Semantic Palettes
- **Canvas Base:** `#F9F9FB` (Frosted Chalk)
- **Surface Elevation 1:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Mineral White)
- **Surface Elevation 2 / Recessed:** `#F2F3F7` (Bleached Stone)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E2E4EB` (Whisper Line)
- **Semantic Status Muted Tones:**
  - *Success / Resolved:* `#7A9A8B` (Faded Lichen)
  - *Warning / In Review:* `#C29F68` (Warm Wheat)
  - *Critical / Escalated:* `#B87070` (Dusty Lingonberry)
  - *Neutral / Pending:* `#8D939F` (Coastal Mist)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy blends the soft, geometric elegance of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles, headers, and UI anchors with the open, humanist rhythm of Source Sans 3 for high-density administrative reading.

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with balanced optical kerning and modest medium-to-semibold weights. Avoid heavy black weights to prevent visual anchoring fatigue.
- **Body Text (Source Sans 3):** Offers wide counters, discernible glyphs for code/ticket identifiers, and comfortable line-height ratios that preserve readability across prolonged ticket logs.
- **Labels & Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Set slightly tracked to retain visual autonomy at sub-14px scales across technical tables, SLA counters, and audit trails.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fluid grid system bounded by generous outer canvas margins, deliberately opposing the edge-to-edge clutter common in legacy IT portals.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, 2.5rem (`40px`) margins. Max container constraint: `1440px` centered to maintain scan lines.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1.5rem (`24px`) margins. Sidebar transforms into a slim icon rail or docked slide-over drawer.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 0.75rem (`12px`) gutters, 1rem (`16px`) margins. Split views stack into a linear vertical feed.

### Spatial Discipline
Internal component layout leverages an unhurried cadence. Card internal padding defaults to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`), allowing critical incident summaries breathing room. Stacks of list items prioritize `space-md` separation over single-pixel micro gaps, minimizing accidental misclicks during high-pressure maintenance windows.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering rejects synthetic neon drop-shadows and thick dark drop-offs in favor of quiet, tonal Scandinavian layering:

- **Flat Tonal Ground:** The canvas rests on `#F9F9FB`. Primary cards sit at Elevation 1 on `#FFFFFF`, delineated by a 1px border of `#E2E4EB` without any shadow in default resting states.
- **Ambient Light Shadow (Interactive / Floating Only):** Popovers, dropdown menus, and elevated dialogs use a diffused, low-opacity ambient drop: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(94, 108, 158, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(108, 114, 122, 0.04)`. The shadow is tinted with the primary slate-periwinkle tone, echoing soft daylight diffusion.
- **Surface Nesting:** Nested data panes (e.g., ticket responses, system logs, code snippets) drop down one tier to an inset tone of `#F2F3F7` with matching hairline borders, preserving containment without stacking visual weight.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, architectural corner geometry (Level 1):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `0.25rem` (`4px`) to preserve structural clarity and data density alignment.
- **Cards & Data Panels:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) via `rounded-lg`, yielding a tailored, calm silhouette that avoids the playfulness of heavy pills while shedding the clinical harshness of sharp corners.
- **Modals & Flyouts:** `0.75rem` (`12px`) via `rounded-xl`, providing a gentle, natural enclosure for focused user flows.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#5E6C9E`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none. Hover state shifts gently to `#525F8C` with no harsh scale transitions.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Background `#F2F3F7`, text `#5E6C9E`, border `1px solid #E2E4EB`. Hover state applies pure white background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FDF3F3`, text `#B87070`, border `1px solid #F1D4D4`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** White background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E2E4EB`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem` (`body-md`). Focused state swaps the border to `#5E6C9E` accompanied by a soft `0 0 0 3px rgba(94, 108, 158, 0.12)` ambient halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimal `16px` enclosures in `#FFFFFF` with a `1.5px` border of `#CAD0DC`. Checked state fills with `#5E6C9E` bearing a crisp white check glyph.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Muted pill tags combining a light-tint background with a deep harmonic text tone:
  - *Open / Active:* Background `#ECEFF7`, Text `#48547E`
  - *Resolved / Stable:* Background `#EBF3EF`, Text `#466958`
  - *Pending / Staged:* Background `#FAF4EB`, Text `#8C6C38`
  - *Urgent / Blocker:* Background `#FDF0F0`, Text `#934545`

### Cards & Ticket Lists
- Plain white container (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E2E4EB`, `0.5rem` radius. Header areas demarcate metadata via quiet `#8D939F` typography. Hovering an interactive list row introduces a soft wash of `#F6F7FA` rather than sharp highlight lines.

### Incident Detail Rails & Drawers
- Multi-pane ticket workbenches use quiet divider borders (`#E2E4EB`) with generous gutters (`1.5rem`). Activity timelines feature a continuous `1px` vertical thread in `#E2E4EB` accented by soft, unlit circular nodes (`#CAD0DC`).
